Privacy Policy

App: kozimovich_bot · Instagram account: @kozimovich_ · Last updated: November 26, 2026

kozimovich_bot is an automated assistant that answers Direct messages and comments sent to the Instagram account @kozimovich_ (operated by Mirsolih Qobulov). This policy explains what data the assistant processes and why.

1. Data we process

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID and username.
  • The text of messages you send to @kozimovich_ in Direct, and comments you leave on @kozimovich_ posts.
  • Images you choose to send in Direct (used only to understand your message).
  • Your name and phone number, only if you write them to us yourself.

We do not access your password, your followers, your other conversations or any data outside your conversation with @kozimovich_.

2. How we use the data

  • To reply to your messages and comments, and to send the materials you request (for example, links to guides).
  • To keep the context of the conversation so replies make sense.
  • If you ask for a service or leave your phone number, to notify the account owner so he can contact you.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not send you messages you did not ask for.

3. Service providers

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram API) — to receive and send messages.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to write the text of replies. Message text is sent to the API to generate a reply and is not used to train models.
  • Telegram — to deliver notifications about new requests to the account owner.

4. Storage and retention

Conversation history and requests are stored in a private database on our server, accessible only to the account owner. Conversation history is kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

5. Deleting your data

To delete all data we hold about you, send the word DELETE in Direct to @kozimovich_. Your data will be deleted within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation in Direct.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: Direct message to @kozimovich_ on Instagram.
